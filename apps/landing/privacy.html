Privacy policy

NetNet keeps where you stand and gives the facts to your own AI agent. This page says what that means for your data: what is held, what never reaches us, who processes it, and how to take it with you or delete it.

In short

  • Your data is stored in Singapore, encrypted at rest and in transit.
  • It is never sold, and NetNet has no advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • Statements and documents you give your agent never reach NetNet.
  • You can export everything at any time.
  • Delete your account and your data is erased at once; our backups expire within 30 days.

What NetNet holds

  • Your account: your email address and either a password hash or the link to the provider you signed in with (Google or Apple).
  • Your profile: your country, your time zone and the segment you chose when you started.
  • The balance sheet you or your agent enter: holdings, values with their currencies and rates, types, tags, accessibility, and the full value history.
  • Your agents: each connection's client name and the times it was started, approved and last used, so the Agent tab and Activity can show them.
  • Notifications: the push endpoints of the devices you turned notifications on for, and your reminder settings and state.
  • Analytics events: names, counts, timings and a user id only. They never carry an amount, a holding name or any text you wrote.
  • Logs and error reports: a request id, route, status, latency, user id, error code and similar technical fields, scrubbed of content.

What NetNet never receives

  • Your documents and statements. Your agent reads a statement in your own client and sends NetNet the figures; the document itself never reaches us.
  • Your data sold or shared for marketing. NetNet has no ads, no tracking SDKs and no session replay.
  • Amounts, names or text in analytics, logs or error reports. Events are typed, and every message NetNet sends you is built from typed fields: no amount, total, holding, type or tag name.

Where it is kept, and who processes it

NetNet's database and its server function run with Neon and Vercel in Singapore. Static files, this page included, are served from Vercel's CDN. Storage is encrypted at rest and traffic is encrypted in transit.

The processors are:

  • Vercel, for hosting and the CDN.
  • Neon, for Postgres.
  • Resend, for email: address verification, password reset, the security email and reminder email.
  • Sentry, for error reports, with default personal data off and bodies, query strings and breadcrumb text dropped.
  • Your browser's own push service, for a reminder when you have notifications on. Which company that is depends on your browser and device.
  • Google and Apple, only when you choose to sign in with them.

Your agent

A connection is made only when you approve it on NetNet's own page, from an account whose email is verified. A connected agent can read your balance sheet and write to it, the same things the web app can do, and every change it makes is in Activity.

Every connection sends a security email to your account address: which agent connected, when, and what it may do, with a link to revoke it. It is email only, never push, it carries no financial data, and it cannot be turned off. You can revoke any connection at any time; later calls from it are rejected and the revocation is in the audit log.

Cookies and analytics

The app sets one first-party session cookie. It is HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite=Lax, and it lasts 30 days of use (sliding). It is how the server knows which account a request belongs to.

This page sets no cookie. The landing page sets none either and stores nothing in your browser: a visit counts through a hash the server computes from the request and a daily salt, and that day's salt is deleted once the day has passed, so a past day's counts cannot be traced back to a person.

There are no advertising or tracking cookies and no third-party analytics SDK.

Reminders

If you have not checked NetNet for 30 days, NetNet may send one reminder, then at most once a month up to three times (days 30, 60 and 90), in the daytime in your own time zone. It goes by push to the devices where notifications are on, and by email only when none is, or every push fails, to a verified address with email reminders on.

A reminder never carries an amount, a total, a holding, type or tag name. Turn reminders off in Settings → Notifications and nothing is sent by any channel. Turn email reminders off and no reminder email is sent. Every reminder email has a one-click unsubscribe. Checking NetNet, in the app or through your agent, restarts the clock.

Export and deletion

Export at any time and you get a complete, machine-readable file: holdings, types, tags, accessibility and the full value history with currencies and rates, enough to rebuild a spreadsheet from.

Delete your account and the deletion happens at once: your ledger rows, account and sessions, agent grants (which revokes every connection), notification endpoints and reminder state go, and your analytics events are re-keyed to a random id, so they still count but no longer point at you. Our daily backups expire within 30 days; that is the stated window, and the app shows it before you confirm.

Security

Each account's data is isolated by Postgres row-level security, so the database itself, not only the application, refuses another account's rows. Passwords are hashed with scrypt. Traffic is over TLS, and responses carry a strict content security policy, HSTS and the related headers.

If you think someone else has used your account, revoke the connection in the Agent tab and change your password.

Who runs NetNet, and how to reach us

NetNet is run by its founder, and the service and its data are in Singapore.

The operator's legal name and registered address, and the contact address for privacy questions, are not in this draft. They will appear here before this policy is final. No reviewer and no review date are named yet, because neither has been recorded.

Where the reviewer should look

Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act is the starting point: the operator and the data are in Singapore. The reviewer should confirm at least:

  • that the PDPA's consent, notification, access and correction, retention and transfer rules are met as written, including the 30-day backup window;
  • whether users in the EU or UK make a GDPR representative or a different lawful basis necessary, and what the transfer wording should be;
  • whether any other launch market's rules need a separate notice, and whether the processor list needs a data-processing agreement with each processor.
  • the operator's legal name, registered address and the contact address, which the Founder supplies.
