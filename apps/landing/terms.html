Terms of use

These terms cover your account, what NetNet does with your data, what your agent may do, and what we each promise. They are deliberately plain, and short enough to read.

In short

  • NetNet is free today, and what is free stays free.
  • NetNet states facts about your position and never gives advice. The advice is your agent's, and your agent is yours.
  • Your data is yours: export it any time, and delete it and it is erased.
  • The service is provided as it is.
  • These terms change when the review is recorded.

Who we are

NetNet is run by its founder, and the service and its data are in Singapore.

The operator's legal name and registered address, and the contact address for questions about these terms, are not in this draft. They will appear here before these terms are final. No reviewer and no review date are named yet, because neither has been recorded.

What NetNet is

NetNet is a context layer for your own AI agent. It keeps the balance sheet you or your agent enter, does the arithmetic (what you could use, what is locked, your currency exposure, what changed, how stale each value is) and gives the facts to the agent you connect.

NetNet states what is true and flags what is uncertain. It does not tell you what to do. NetNet is not a financial adviser, and a question about what you should do belongs with your agent.

Your agent is a third party you choose. Its availability, its own terms and the answers it gives are its own, not NetNet's, and NetNet does not control or endorse them.

Your account

Use your own account and an email address you can receive mail at. Keep your password to yourself; activity under your account is yours. An agent can be connected only from a verified address, with your approval on NetNet's own page.

If you think someone else has used your account, revoke the connection in the Agent tab and change your password.

Free, and what stays free

Everything NetNet does today is free, including your agent's reads and writes through MCP, uncapped. What is free stays free for those users.

Some things are deliberately reserved and are not built in v1: automated market-price and FX refresh, server-initiated agent updates, NetNet-built forecasting and scenario planning, tax and year-end reporting, shared or household portfolios, a document vault, and threshold alerts. Your agent may still model, project or reason about tax from what it reads; that is your agent's work, not a NetNet feature.

Your data

Your data is yours. NetNet holds and processes it only to run the service for you: to store it, compute the figures, hand them to the agent you connected, send the messages you asked for, and keep the audit log.

NetNet does not sell your data or share it for marketing. Export everything at any time. Delete your account and it is erased at once, with backups expiring within 30 days. The privacy policy says exactly what is held and for how long.

Your agent, and what a connection may do

Approving a connection lets that agent read and write your balance sheet, the same as the web app. Every change it makes is in Activity, and you can revoke the connection at any time; later calls from it are rejected. Every connection sends a security email that cannot be turned off.

An agent registers a name with NetNet. NetNet cannot verify that name, and says so when it shows it.

Acceptable use

  • Do not use NetNet to break the law, to harm someone, or to reach another account's data.
  • Do not probe, overload or bypass the service's limits, and do not misrepresent NetNet to anyone else.
  • Do not enter someone else's financial data without their permission.

NetNet may suspend an account that does these things.

The service as it is

NetNet is provided as it is. It does not promise to be uninterrupted or free of errors. The figures depend on what you or your agent enter and on the exchange rates NetNet fetches, so check anything you rely on.

NetNet is not responsible for decisions you or your agent make from the facts it supplies, to the extent the law allows.

Ending it

You can delete your account at any time, and the privacy policy describes what that erases. NetNet can end access for a breach of acceptable use.
