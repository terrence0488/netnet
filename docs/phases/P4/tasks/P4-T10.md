# P4-T10: The launch note

Status: todo
Depends on: P4-T2 (ordering: the note quotes the landing page as shipped)
Parallel-safe: yes

## Goal

One launch note the Founder can post, without rewriting it, to three or more communities that
between them reach all three personas, leading with §2.5's statement and varying only the proof to
suit each audience (G-D3).

## Context

- PRD §2.5 and §3.4 G-D3. The note leads with **you can't get useful advice from an AI that doesn't
  know where you stand**, never with "you don't know your net worth". P-B and P-C carry the kill
  criteria (§3.3), so the channel mix must put enough of them in front of the product; a launch that
  recruits mostly P-A gives a large, unreadable result.
- Channel selection and posting are Founder work (§3.4); the note and a shortlist of candidate
  communities with their fit are this task. For each candidate: which personas it reaches and
  roughly how many, its rules on self-promotion and links, the best format and length, and whether
  a link unfurls (P4-T2's Open Graph card). SG and SEA first (§2), with personal-finance, AI-tool
  and MCP communities among them.
- The note states only what is true (the landing page's facts, P4-T2): free, what stays free,
  works with Claude and any agent that supports MCP connectors, facts not advice, data in
  Singapore, never sold, export and delete any time. It invites people in; it does not oversell.
  No figures from anyone's real finances; examples use the demo household (P4-T1).
- One link per post, to the landing page, untagged: the landing page counts visitors only, and
  nothing in NetNet reads where a visitor came from (G-D4, N9).

## Interface contract

- `ops/launch/launch-note.md` (new):
  - the note: a title, the lead line, a short body (what NetNet is, what the agent does with it,
    the trust facts, the link), under 200 words;
  - three proofs, each a paragraph that slots in after the lead: for someone who has never added it
    up (P-A), for someone whose spreadsheet goes stale (P-B), for someone who already pastes their
    spreadsheet into Claude (P-C);
  - per candidate community: which proof, which format (post, comment, thread), its rules, and the
    note as posted there, ready to paste;
  - a reply sheet: short, true answers to the questions P-B and P-C ask first (where is my data,
    is it sold, what can the agent change, how do I delete it, why is it free).
