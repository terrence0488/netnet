# P4-T6: All ten packs reviewed

Status: todo
Depends on: P4-T5 (the drafts and the review record); F20 (a reviewer per market)
Parallel-safe: yes (one small change per pack, as each review comes back)

## Goal

Each of the ten launch packs (SG, MY, ID, PH, TH, VN, HK, IN, AU, GB) has been read by someone who
knows that market, their corrections are in, the pack is live for its country, and the review is
recorded against the exact document that was reviewed.

## Context

- PRD §2.3 rule 2 (a wrong pack is worse than no pack) and CONVENTIONS §8. P4-T5 built the record
  (`content/reviews.json`) and the publish guard that refuses an unreviewed pack in production.
- The Founder engages the reviewers (F3, F20) and sends each one the pack in a readable form: the
  checklist as the basics ask it, each type's label, default, reason and question, with the sources
  from P4-T5's PR. The engineer prepares that form per pack (`pnpm content:review-sheet <pack>`,
  plain text or Markdown, no code) so the reviewer never reads JSON.
- One change per pack, on its own branch (`p4-t6-sg`, `p4-t6-id`, …), each:
  1. applies the reviewer's corrections;
  2. for the seven new markets, moves the pack from `content/packs/drafts/` to `content/packs/` and
     sets `pack` on its country in `content/countries.json`;
  3. adds the review record (the reviewed document's hash, the reviewer as they agree to be named,
     the date, any notes) and a `Reviewed-by:` trailer on the commit.
  A reviewer's correction that the schema cannot express, or that touches a universal type, comes
  back to the Founder as a question, not a schema change.
- A pack edit never reclassifies an existing holding (§2.3): labels follow the new version,
  outcomes and reasons stay as applied. The SG, MY and GB packs are live with people's holdings on
  them, so their reviews change future defaults only; say so to the reviewer.
- The production deploy publishes each reviewed pack (`deploy.yml` runs `content:publish --env
  production --strict`). P3's test report noted that an SG car-loan type reads "Loan" after a move
  to GB; that follows the PRD and stays.

## Interface contract

- `scripts/content-review-sheet.ts`, `pnpm content:review-sheet <pack>`: prints the pack as a
  reviewer reads it, merged over generic as the product resolves it.
- Per pack: the moved or corrected `content/packs/<id>.json`, the `countries.json` line, the
  `content/reviews.json` entry.
