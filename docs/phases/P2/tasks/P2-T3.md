# P2-T3: Chart geometry and SVG components

Status: in-progress
Depends on: none
Parallel-safe: yes

## Goal

Pure chart geometry in `core.chart` and accessible SVG components for the trend, allocation and
assets-vs-liabilities charts, plus the frosted "Need more data" placeholder.

## Context

No charting library: d3 maths and our own SVG, geometry shared with native later (architecture
§4 Stack, "Charts"; §7.9 `packages/core`). The design system is the spec and the prototype the
behavioural reference (CONVENTIONS §13); binding rules: the categorical palette in fixed order,
brand and chart hues never sharing a role, 44 px touch targets (36 px with a fine pointer),
reduced motion honoured, tokens only. PRD: J4 (reading the trend, the placeholder).

Consumers are P2-T4 (Home) and P2-T5 (holding page); this task delivers the geometry and the
components with hooks (`data-testid`, roles) their Playwright specs drive.

## Interface contract

- `core.chart`: `{scales, linePath, bars, stacked, ticks(n, width), nearestPoint(x)}`
  (architecture §7.9). Pure: no clock, randomness or I/O; `today` passed in (CONVENTIONS §3, §5).
- Trend reading: hover, tap, drag and arrow keys read the date and value at the point, every
  series on a multi-series chart; a plain swipe still scrolls and never traps the page (PRD J4).
- Placeholder: keeps the chart's place as a sample of its shape in neutral ink under frosted
  glass, labelled "Need more data", with one line saying what makes it appear (a later update,
  or a value with an earlier date); no figures, no dates, no data colours (PRD J4).

## Files in scope

- `packages/core/src/**` (chart geometry only)
- `apps/web/src/charts/**` (SVG components only)
- Unit tests for the geometry

## Done criteria
