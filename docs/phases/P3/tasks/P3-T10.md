## Goal

P3's trust, comeback and Insights work holds together on a phone and a
computer, every new event fires with its segment, and the phase lands on
production ready for Stage 6.

## Context

- The P3 checkpoint is §4's P3 section in `docs/04-plan.md`: Activity with
  undo, settings, export and deletion, the reminder by push or email with the
  security-email retries, and Insights on the web and through the agent.
- Every phase ends on production, on the Founder's domain, deployed by the
  manual workflow (architecture §9.2). Until launch, production is unannounced
  and `/` is a holding page.
- Push cannot be fully tested headless (architecture §12 risk 16): this task
  verifies what automation can and hands the rest to Stage 6's manual checks on
  a real iPhone (NetNet on the Home Screen) and an Android phone.
- A preview's `DATABASE_URL` is injected per-deployment by its Preview job:
  never redeploy from the Vercel dashboard; re-run the Preview job instead.

## Interface contract

- No new product surface. This task runs the matrix below, fixes what it finds
  within the P3 briefs' scope (anything outside comes back as a P3 follow-up
  brief, not an improvisation), and deploys.
- Matrix: each P3 flow's E2E at 390 px and 1280 px; no horizontal scroll at
  320 px; axe on Activity, Settings, Notifications, Insights and the holding
  chart; analytics audit (`value_undone`, `reminder_sent/opened`,
  `push_prompt_shown/result`, `push_enabled/disabled`, `account_deleted`,
  `insights_viewed` — each with `segment`, none carrying amounts or names);
  the N10 deny-list scan; the privacy tests (every `notify()` message free of
  digits-with-currency and holding names).
- Production verification after deploy: the checkpoint items on
  `https://‹domain›`, the three cron entries present, the reminder claim
  working across runs, the 60-a-day cap in place.
