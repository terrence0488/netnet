Drop them here with these exact names and they appear in the prototype on reload (Grok's steps
are a generic draft until its connector support is confirmed; Muse's are written from Meta's
launch and early testers' reports, and Muse is US-only for now, so its screenshots need a US
account):

PNG, cropped to the part of the screen the step talks about.

**This folder is committed to the repo.** Before adding a screenshot, crop or blur anything
personal: your name, email, avatar, and any chat titles in the sidebar.

In the product these become content a human admin edits in the admin portal (PRD §9.6
item 17), because Claude's and ChatGPT's own screens change without notice. Until then, they
are files here.
